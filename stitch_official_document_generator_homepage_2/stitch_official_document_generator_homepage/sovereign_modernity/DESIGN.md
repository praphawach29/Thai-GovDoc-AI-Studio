## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the standard of Thai e-Government digital workspace transformation: clean, dignified, highly credible, and intuitive. It harmonizes the strict, authoritative heritage of the Office of the Prime Minister Regulations on Correspondence Clerical Work (ระเบียบสำนักนายกรัฐมนตรีว่าด้วยงานสารบรรณ) with state-of-the-art AI productivity tooling.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Institutional SaaS** with **Tactile Document Clarity**:
- **Dignified & Institutional:** Anchored by sovereign deep teal slate (`#0A3641`) and crisp navy surfaces, inspiring trust and civil service authenticity.
- **AI-Powered Clarity:** Accented with vibrant emerald and mint tones (`#059669`, `#10B981`) that visually signal AI Copilot actions, automatic phrasing, legal validations, and verification engines.
- **Ergonomic Workspace:** Soft neutral canvas tiers (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) isolate eye fatigue during lengthy legal drafting sessions and split-screen document comparisons.
- **Prestigious Details:** Delicate warm gold/amber accents highlight official document status badges, royal seals, and legal verifications.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional authority while maintaining an open, responsive workspace for civil servants and legal officers.

- **Primary (`#0A3641`):** Represents institutional gravitas, stability, and legal integrity. Used for major CTAs, navigation headers, primary active indicators, and high-level headings.
- **Secondary (`#059669`) & AI Accent (`#10B981`):** Emphasizes active intelligence, automatic drafting suggestions, accuracy indicators, and verification badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Reserved for official registry seals, priority levels, pending reviews, and formal Thai government emblem ribbons.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Governs secondary labels, placeholders, breadcrumbs, and subtle borders.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** Layered light foundations providing soft contrast against document canvas sheets and input fields without harsh screen glare.
- **Urgency Accents:** Dedicated reds (`#DC2626`) and ambers reserved exclusively for royal urgency classes (*ด่วนที่สุด*, *ด่วนมาก*, *ด่วน*).

## Typography

The interface bifurcates typographic rules between the **SaaS Digital Workspace UI** and the **Official Document Print Sandbox**:

1. **Digital UI Workspace:**
   - **Headings & Action Labels (`Prompt`):** Provides contemporary, geometric Thai character structures with exceptional readability at small sizes, eliminating the sterile look of legacy software while maintaining strict decorum.
   - **Body & Data Content (`Inter`):** Paired for numerical precision, document counts, Latin references, and system messages.

2. **Official Document Canvas Sandbox (Preview & Export Engine):**
   - Strictly renders in **TH Sarabun New** with zero-width non-breaking spaces (`\u200b`) between syllables and justified alignment (`Thai Distributed`) to guarantee legal formatting compliance and prevent floating diacritics/vowels (*สระลอย*).
   - Indentation for memo bodies conforms strictly to standard tab stops (2.5 cm, 3.5 cm, 4.5 cm).

## Layout & Spacing

The UI relies on a multi-pane layout structured around rapid composition, OCR ingest, and legal validation:

- **Sidebar Navigation:** Fixed `260px` sidebar containing hierarchical government department selectors, navigation links, and identity switches. Collapses to a compact icon bar (`64px`) on tablet screens.
- **Split-Screen Workspace:** When comparing source evidence with drafts, the viewport utilizes a balanced `50/50` fluid grid split with a `24px` gutter and independent scroll containers.
- **Studio Home & Prompt Canvas:** Center-aligned maximum width container (`max-w-4xl`) providing an unencumbered, distraction-free environment for document instruction inputs and template triggers.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Full-width stacked layout, drawer sidebar, floating action bars pinned to bottom.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Single-column editor with tab toggles between "Original Document" and "AI Draft".
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full multi-column workspace with persistent left navigation and dual-pane comparative editor.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages ambient, low-contrast shadows and clean surface separation rather than heavy skeuomorphism:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base App Background):** `#F8FAFC` — Matte, clean light slate base.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Sidebars, Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px` stroke in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Prompt Input Box):** Elevated slightly with a soft, diffused ambient drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(10, 54, 65, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 54, 65, 0.03)`), giving interactive focus to the AI command center.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns):** Defined by `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **A4 Document Simulation Canvas:** Displays a subtle dimensional outline with pure flat vector borders representing physical paper edges.

## Shapes

The interface embraces modern ergonomic curves while respecting document rectilinear structure:
- **Interactive Buttons & Controls:** Rounded-lg (`8px`) to rounded-xl (`12px`) providing soft, contemporary touch targets.
- **Floating AI Prompt Box & Content Cards:** Generous `16px` to `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) corners to present the AI studio as an approachable, cutting-edge assistant.
- **Document Export & Preview Canvases:** Explicitly sharp (`0px` or `2px`) to honor genuine physical paper and ISO A4 dimensions.
- **Badges & Filter Chips:** Rounded-full pill shapes for quick category browsing and workflow tag toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary AI Action:** Solid `#0A3641` background with white text, transitioning to `#0E4B5B` on hover; accompanied by crisp emerald accent pips for active generation states.
- **Secondary / Draft Actions:** White background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0A3641` text with subtle `#F8FAFC` hover fill.
- **Send / Generate Floating Button:** Pill or circular `#0A3641` button with smooth arrow/sparkle icon, auto-activating to vibrant emerald `#059669` when prompt text is present.

### Floating AI Prompt Box
- Prominently positioned center-stage with `rounded-2xl`, `#FFFFFF` surface, and `#E2E8F0` hairline border.
- Integrated file attachment clip, tone adjustment toggle, and prompt presets ribbon arranged below the primary multiline textarea.

### Chips & Quick Prompts
- Lightweight pill buttons (`rounded-full`) in `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` outlines and `#475569` text.
- Feature emoji or SVG icons denoting common actions (e.g., `บันทึกข้อความ`, `หนังสือภายนอก`, `คลังแบบฟอร์ม`, `คลังกฎหมาย`).
- Active/selected state switches to `#ECFDF5` background with `#059669` border and text.

### Split Document Viewer & Editor
- **Left Panel (Original Source):** High-fidelity document viewer with OCR bounding boxes and text inspection highlights.
- **Right Panel (AI Copilot Drafting Canvas):** Editable 12-block form architecture with smart editable tokens (`[✏️ ...]`) for manual parameter overrides.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Verification States:**
  - *พบ (Verified):* `#ECFDF5` fill with `#059669` text and icon.
  - *ต้องยืนยัน (Needs Review):* `#FEF3C7` fill with `#B45309` text.
  - *ไม่พบ (Missing):* `#FEF2F2` fill with `#DC2626` text.
- **Government Urgency Seals:** High-visibility red bordered pill stamps (*ด่วนที่สุด*, *ด่วนมาก*) adhering to royal office directives.

### Sidebar Nav Items
- Clean vertical items with `rounded-xl` shape, `#64748B` neutral tone, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` hover.
- Selected state adopts `#0A3641` background with `#FFFFFF` text or subtle light-teal highlight with an active vertical pill pip.
